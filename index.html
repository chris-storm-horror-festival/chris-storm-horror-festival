About the Festival

Chris Storm Independent Horror Festival – Vol. 1 is a fully online horror short film festival for independent filmmakers from around the world.

We celebrate no-budget, low-budget and independent productions. What matters is ideas, atmosphere, storytelling and a personal cinematic vision.

NO BUDGET. LOW BUDGET. INDEPENDENT HORROR.
YOUR FILM. YOUR NIGHTMARE. YOUR VISION.

Rules & Terms

  • Horror short films only; maximum 30 minutes including credits.
  • No-budget, low-budget and independent productions are welcome.
  • No premiere requirement.
  • Non-English films require English subtitles.
  • The submitter must hold the necessary rights.
  • Submission does not guarantee selection; filmmakers retain ownership.
